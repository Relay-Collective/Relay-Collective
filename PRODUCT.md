# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: front-desk staff at housing and social-service organizations (the pilot partner is Belkin House, with The Salvation Army as pilot partner, not owner). They are mid-task, often on a phone, booking a ride for a person they support.

Secondary: volunteer drivers who accept rides on their own hours, area and vehicle; driver-provider org admins; Relay Collective platform admins.

Clients (the riders) are not users. They need no phone, app, account or money.

## Product Purpose

A free, not-for-profit network that turns phoning around for rides into one shared request, passed along until a driver accepts and kept in view until the person arrives. Success: the person gets to the appointment that matters (hospital, housing interview, clinic, food bank) and staff can see it happened.

## Positioning

Staff book on the client's behalf, so the rider needs no smartphone, app or payment. Requests are matched on service area, request hours, seats and wheelchair access; first driver to accept takes the ride; a return ride is one tap and asks the same driver first. Promise: "Rides that show up. Today."

## Operating Context

Staff on phones at a front desk; drivers on old phones, often outdoors. Printable large-text ride slips for clients without a phone. Web app wrapped with Capacitor for iOS and Android.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Statuses: searching, offered, accepted, picked up, completed, needs attention (no driver available), no-show, cancelled (including sent on transit). Staff can rate finished rides, drivers can flag issues, every ride keeps a change history. Never store more about a client than the driver needs (an optional first name).

## Brand Commitments

Independent third party, inspired by but not copying Salvation Army branding: no shield, crest, wordmark, exact red-yellow pairing or faith framing. Blue/aqua heart-road logo is the identity. Sentence case, plain speech. See DESIGN.md.

## Evidence on Hand

README, PRODUCT_DESCRIPTION.md, USER_TESTING_FEEDBACK.md, PARTNER_ORG_NOTES.md. No testimonials or usage numbers to invent beyond those documents.

## Product Principles

1. Clear over clever: one main action per screen.
2. Dignity first: the client is never a "case"; no sad imagery.
3. Status is obvious and never colour alone.
4. Nothing slips through: an unfilled ride is surfaced immediately.
5. Accessible by default: WCAG 2.2 AA, large text, old phones.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA minimum; works outdoors, on old phones, with large text.
